Colors

All the colors of the Birman cat

The Birman is a “colorpoint” cat: the body stays pale and the color is concentrated on the mask, ears, legs and tail. Whatever its coat, it always keeps its two signatures: sapphire-blue eyes and four white-gloved paws. The Birman comes in 40 recognized colors. Here are the main ones, illustrated by our own cats.

What never changesBlue eyes, always. Four white gloves. A pale body with colored points.
What does not exist in the BirmanGreen or golden eyes, bicolor or white-faced coats, solid coats without points. A cat like this is not a Birman.
TortieIt mixes a color with red. So it does not exist in red or cream, and occurs almost only in females.
A color that depends on temperatureThe colorpoint gene only colors the coolest parts of the body: mask, ears, legs and tail. The body stays eggshell, the back golden beige. Kittens are born almost white, and their points darken over the weeks.
Seal, the original colorSeal is the Birman’s original color, with a striking contrast. It dilutes to blue, chocolate dilutes to lilac and red to cream. The coat darkens over the years.
Tabby and tortieTabby adds stripes and an “M” on the forehead. Tortie mixes red with another color. Because it is carried by the X chromosome, it occurs almost only in females.
Silver and smokeSilver is a dominant color: at least one parent must be silver. A smoke is recognized by the white at the base of its colored hairs and the white tufts in its ears.

Frequently asked questions

Birman cat colors: your questions

How many colors does the Birman cat come in?

Since FIFe recognized silver tabby and smoke on May 27, 2016, the Birman has 40 colors. All are colorpoint: solid, tabby, tortie, silver and smoke, on seal, blue, chocolate, lilac, red and cream bases. All keep the blue eyes and white gloves.

What is the most common Birman color?

Seal point, very dark brown points on a pale body, is the Birman’s original and best-known color. Blue point and the tabbies are also very common.

How can you tell a Birman kitten’s color?

Kittens are born almost white. Their points start to color within a few days and become clearer over the first weeks. The breeder works out the color from the parents’ colors, then records it on the LOOF pedigree. In smokes, the color can be confusing at first and only becomes clear at around 4 to 6 months.

Can a male Birman be tortie?

Very rarely. The tortie color needs two X chromosomes, which only females have. A tortie male carries a genetic anomaly and is almost always sterile.

Does a Birman’s color change with age?

Yes. A Birman’s coat darkens over the years, especially in seals, and the points grow more intense. The coolest parts of the body can also darken in winter.

What is a silver or smoke Birman?

A Birman that carries the dominant silver gene. A silver tabby has stripes on a silver background. A smoke has white at the base of its colored hairs, lots of white hair in the ears and sometimes faint tabby markings on the mask. These colors come from Chinchilla Persians and were then selected over about ten generations.
