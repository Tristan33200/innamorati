Adopt a kitten

What you are really adopting

Our kittens leave between 15 weeks and 4½ months of age, already spayed or neutered, after months of care, socialization and veterinary follow-up.

€1,800

Pet kitten, everything included

  • LOOF pedigree
  • Already spayed or neutered
  • Microchip identification
  • Vaccinations, deworming and veterinary certificate
  • Tested, closely followed parents
  • Sales contract and information document
  • Photo album and lifelong support
  1. i.
    Getting to know each other

    You write to us. We talk about your home, your lifestyle and the kitten that suits you.

  2. ii.
    Meeting our cats

    You visit us at home in Bordeaux to meet the parents and see how our cats live with the family.

  3. iii.
    Reserving

    A deposit reserves your kitten. You receive its photos week after week.

  4. iv.
    Welcoming your kitten

    Between 15 weeks and 4½ months, spayed or neutered, your kitten joins your home. We are always here for you, and its photos keep growing its album.
